# Draf Materi dari Sesi — Small Talk di Kantor

Level: menengah

Saran penempatan: topik baru: Small talk di kantor

Sumber sesi: 2026-11-25 — memperluas tema Menengah, latihan basa-basi di tempat kerja

## Item

- en: "How was your weekend?"
  meaning: "Bagaimana akhir pekanmu?"
  note: "Pertanyaan basa-basi yang aman di Senin pagi."

- en: "It's been pretty busy lately."
  meaning: "Akhir-akhir ini agak sibuk."
  note: "Jawaban umum yang tidak terlalu pribadi; 'pretty' melemahkan 'busy'."

- en: "Did you get a chance to try that café?"
  meaning: "Sudah sempat coba kafe itu?"
  note: "Lanjutan obrolan yang ramah; 'get a chance to' lebih natural daripada 'have time to'."

## Prompt latihan

You run into a colleague at the coffee machine on Monday morning. Write 3–4 sentences of small talk using at least one of: break the ice / catch up / how's it going.

## Kunci jawaban statis

Morning! How was your weekend? Did you get a chance to try that new café?
