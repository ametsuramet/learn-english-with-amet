# Draf Materi dari Sesi — Menulis Email Tindak Lanjut

Level: menengah

Saran penempatan: topik baru: Menulis email tindak lanjut

Sumber sesi: 2026-11-25 — memperluas tema Menengah, latihan email follow-up profesional

## Item

- en: "Just following up on our conversation."
  meaning: "Sekadar menindaklanjuti percakapan kita."
  note: "Pembuka email follow-up yang standar dan tidak terdengar menagih."

- en: "As promised, here's the file."
  meaning: "Seperti yang dijanjikan, ini filenya."
  note: "Menepati janji dengan rapi; 'as promised' lebih natural dari 'like I promised'."

- en: "Let me know if you need anything else."
  meaning: "Kabari kalau butuh apa-apa lagi."
  note: "Penutup yang ramah dan lazim di email kantor."

## Prompt latihan

You promised to send a file yesterday but forgot. Write 3–4 sentences of a follow-up email using at least one of: follow up on / circle back / keep someone in the loop.

## Kunci jawaban statis

Hi, just following up on my last message — sorry for the delay, but here's the file as promised. Let me know if you need anything else.
